How to Use

  1. Hold Ctrl + Alt + Z.
  2. Cursor becomes a crosshair (+) — scan mode.
  3. Click and drag to draw a box around the poster/title.
  4. Release mouse — OCR scans the area.
  5. Release the keys — crosshair disappears.
  6. Or just highlight a title anywhere in text — a Rate button appears next to it.

Works on any website. Try first scan. Works offline (Tesseract) unless you enable Gemini OCR.
